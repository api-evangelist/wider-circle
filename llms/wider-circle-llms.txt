## What Wider Circle does

The Connect for Life program clusters health-plan members by claims-derived affinity, recruits and trains local Ambassadors and Facilitators from within those clusters, and runs in-person and virtual group programming. The stated outcomes are annual wellness visit completion, CAHPS and Star Ratings movement, medication adherence, reduced hospitalization, reduced social isolation, and social-determinants-of-health referral. Buyers are health plans and provider groups, not developers.
